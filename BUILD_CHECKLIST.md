## ABOUT PAGE

### Section 1 — About
Add Section → **About** category → text preset. Heading:

```
About 8TPS
```

Body (official medium bio, verbatim):

```
8TPS is a radical vocal collective of trans, nonbinary, and gender-expansive musicians. Known for their playful interpretations, powerful reflections, and polystylistic surprises, this company of genre-fluid performers & educators centers community, artistry, joy, and trans excellence. Working across contemporary a cappella, experimental music, and a variety of folk spaces, 8TPS collaborates with trans and queer artists of all kinds through multiple musical modalities. Past partners include Mariachi Arco•Iris, Tonality, Celebration Theater, The Colony Theatre, Grand Performances, WeHo Pride, and more.
```

### Section 2 — The 8TPS Story
Heading:

```
The 8TPS Story
```

Body (drafted from the longest official bio in the EPK docx — worth a skim against the docx before launch):

```
Founded in December 2023, 8TPS brings together performers whose expertise spans mariachi, shapenote singing, opera, jazz, and musical theater, connecting commercial, traditional, and experimental music making. The ensemble's repertoire ranges from pop a cappella to contemporary classical and folk traditions.

8TPS is committed to expanding the canon of trans-composed works, commissioning and premiering new music through projects like the Never Change music video and the concert Inventing/Inviting: Trans Songs of Change. The ensemble has performed at venues including the LA LGBT Center, The Colony Theatre, the Muckenthaler Cultural Center, Grand Performances, WeHo City Council Chambers, Hudson Theatres, and Human Resources, alongside collaborators such as Mariachi Arco•Iris, Tonality, Urban Voices Project, Strong Words: Voices of the City, and Yozmit the DogStar.
```
