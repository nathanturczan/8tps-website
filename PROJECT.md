## About 8TPS

**Full Name**: 8TPS (pronounced "eight-tips" or similar)
**Type**: A cappella-forward octet / trans choral collective
**Location**: Los Angeles, California
**Founded**: December 2023
**Tagline**: "Community. Artistry. Joy. Trans Excellence."

### Description

8TPS is a polystylistic, a cappella-forward group at the intersection of professional, identity-driven, and community-building choral ensembles. A company of genre-fluid performers, adaptable arts educators, and energized community partners, 8TPS embodies the diversity of trans experience and excellence.

They are a trans choral collective of trans and gender-expansive musicians in Los Angeles and Southern California, comprising trans, nonbinary, and gender-expansive musicians.
