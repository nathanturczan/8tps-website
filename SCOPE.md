# 8TPS Website Scope

All items required before official launch.

### 1.1 Landing Page

- **Heading**: "Community. Artistry. Joy. Trans Excellence."
- **Featured Production Section**:
  - If launch before August 2026: **p h o r i a**
  - If launch August or later: **Queer History Mixtape**

- **Social Icons**: Instagram | YouTube | Facebook
- **Logo**: Footer placement

## Phase 3: Long-Term (2027+)

- **Store/Merch Page** - "Merch with a message" > logo merch
- **Sheet Music Page** - Links to purchase published arrangements, composer websites

---

## Out of Scope

Items explicitly excluded or to avoid:

- Heavy/cumbersome navigation bars
- Header images that block body text (must see text without scrolling)
- Animated section changes
- Fade-in text effects
- Infinite scroll / continuous loading
- Low-contrast colors
- Pop-up newsletter modals
- Inaccessible design patterns
