## Section 1: About

### Headline

```
About 8TPS
```

### Main Description

*Draft based on research - client to review/revise:*

```
8TPS is a polystylistic, a cappella-forward octet at the intersection of
professional, identity-driven, and community-building choral ensembles.

A company of genre-fluid performers, adaptable arts educators, and energized
community partners, 8TPS embodies the diversity of trans experience and excellence.

We are a trans choral collective of trans and gender-expansive musicians in
Los Angeles and Southern California, comprising trans, nonbinary, and
gender-expansive musicians.
```
