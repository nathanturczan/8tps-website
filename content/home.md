## Featured Production

### Current: p h o r i a (if launching before August 2026)

*Scraped from Instagram (@8tps.music), tannerpfeiffer.com/8tps, and Ticket Tailor on 2026-07-22.*

**Title**: p h o r i a — a musical anthology
(poster subtitle: "A Trans Musical Anthology")

**Dates**: Saturday, August 8, 2026, 7:30 PM – 9:00 PM

**Venue**: The Renberg Theatre, LA LGBT Center, 1125 N McCadden Pl, Los Angeles, CA 90038

**Description** (official copy from announcement post / Ticket Tailor):
> On August 8th, join 8TPS for p h o r i a, a musical anthology of new works by trans composers. Featuring six world premieres performed by an all-trans string quartet and vocal octet, this concert offers a new lexicon of trans experience expressed through an array of musical styles. Don't miss an unforgettable selection of songs about belonging, identity, joy, and more!
>
> This program is supported in part by an arts grant from the City of West Hollywood.

**World premieres by**: Anaís Azul, Aria Gittelson, Wells Leng, Tanner Pfeiffer, Yaya Vasquez-Lopez, Socks Whitmore

**Access note**: Paid parking in the LGBT Center's garage; street parking nearby. The Renberg Theatre is wheelchair accessible.

**Image**: `assets/phoria/phoria-poster.jpg` (full-res 3274×4096, from Instagram announcement post)

**Ticket Link**: https://www.tickettailor.com/events/8tpsmusic/2205578 (short link: bit.ly/phoria2026)

**Bonus content** (from the promo campaign, usable for a production page):
- "Lexicon" series: each premiere is named for an invented word — plerophoria (Leng), apistophoria (Gittelson), anikophoria (Pfeiffer), linguaphoria (Vasquez-Lopez), anthesphoria (Azul), ipsophoria (Whitmore) — each with a definition and etymology
- Composer spotlight posts with bios for all six composers
