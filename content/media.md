## Photo Gallery

### Required Photos (from EPK)

1. **Ensemble Group Photo** - Full group, professional setting
2. **Performance Photos** - Action shots during performances
3. **Venue Photos** - 8TPS at notable venues
4. **Community Photos** - Outreach events, workshops
5. **Behind-the-Scenes** - Rehearsals, candid moments

### Photo Specifications
- High resolution (minimum 2000px wide)
- Landscape orientation preferred for headers
- Include photographer credit
- Provide alt text descriptions for accessibility

### Alt Text Examples
```
Eight singers performing on stage at The Colony Theatre, dressed in matching black attire
```
```
8TPS ensemble rehearsing in a circle, scripts in hand
```
